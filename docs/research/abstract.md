# Abstract

Humor detection in code-mixed languages like Hindi-English poses a significant challenge due to the complex interplay of cultural context, linguistic nuances, and multimodal presentation (text and image). Traditional natural language processing models struggle to grasp the humor without access to the visual modalities, while purely visual models lack the cultural and linguistic understanding required to parse the text. This project introduces a novel approach leveraging Vision-Language Models (VLMs) augmented with a Cultural Retrieval mechanism. By injecting cultural context into the VLM prompts (Mode B) compared to standard zero-shot VLM inference (Mode A), we demonstrate a structured methodology for evaluating humor classification on the Memotion 3 dataset. Our system emphasizes scientific rigor, ensuring metrics are derived strictly from empirical experimentation without fabrication, and establishes a robust baseline for future research in multimodal, cross-cultural humor analysis.
